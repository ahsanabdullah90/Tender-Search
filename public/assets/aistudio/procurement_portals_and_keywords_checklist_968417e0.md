## 7. Daily Workflow Checklist

- [ ] **Step 1:** Query Federal PPRA (EPADS) and Provincial PPRAs (Punjab, Sindh, KP, Balochistan) using keywords 06, 07, 08, 18, and 19.
- [ ] **Step 2:** Check multilateral boards (UNGM and GIZ) for education, vocational training, and development RFPs.
- [ ] **Step 3:** Review autonomous bodies (PSEB, Ignite, PSDF, PSDA, PEEF) for specialized bids.
- [ ] **Step 4:** Cross-check aggregator alerts (Tender Services Pakistan) and daily newspaper e-papers for tender corrigenda/extensions.
